PROJECT: FairShed, a fair and auditable load-shedding planner (Energy & Smart Infrastructure domain).

CONTEXT (use for the problem statement; do not invent more numbers): Pakistan's power shortfall exceeded 4,000 MW in Oct 2026 with 3-4 hour cuts. In Sept 2026 the regulator Nepra found K-Electric's outages on 620 feeders frequently exceeded its own limits and banned loss-based feeder cuts. A federal committee is reviewing a load-shedding exemption for telecom towers; express feeders already protect hospitals and water pumps. Real feeder maps are not public, so use synthetic feeders on illustrative Karachi zones and label them synthetic everywhere.

PROBLEM: N feeders, T hours. In hour t exactly m_t feeders are off (m_t comes from the forecast shortfall and differs per hour; feeders are equal-size so the quota is a count). Protected feeders (hospital, water pump, telecom tower) are never off. x[t,f]=1 means feeder f is off in hour t; qubit index = t*N + f. Minimize: hardship (per-feeder per-hour harm from heat/population) + fairness term (uneven total outage hours, using a ledger of hours already cut) + consecutive-outage penalty (same feeder off in adjacent hours). Hard constraints: per-hour quota, protected exclusions. Base instance 8 feeders x 3 hours with quotas 3, 2, 4 (feeder 2 = hospital, feeder 6 = water pump); keep QAOA runs at 16-20 qubits; support up to 10x2. Receding-horizon re-planning each hour, warm-started from the previous plan.

QUANTUM DESIGN: (1) Dicke state per hour block over non-protected qubits plus an XY mixer inside each block. (2) Grover-mixer QAOA reusing the same Dicke preparation: mixer = U_S (I - (1 - e^{-i*beta})|0><0|) U_S^dagger. (3) Penalty-QAOA with the standard X mixer as a weak baseline. (4) Quantum counting / iterative amplitude estimation of the number of valid plans on a small instance, verified by exact enumeration (the 'flexibility' number). (5) Amplitude estimation for CVaR / tail risk of outage on critical feeders, on a 4-6 feeder block, using a shortfall scenario distribution from the forecast residuals. (6) GroverOptimizer (Grover Adaptive Search) on a ~8-variable block as comparator. (7) Aer MPS simulator scaling curve: approximation ratio vs bond dimension vs number of feeders, versus the exact classical answer.

CLASSICAL AND DATA: exact brute force over valid plans (MILP if easy), round-robin and random-valid baselines, and at least one scenario where round-robin and random clearly fail (unequal hardship plus a consecutive-outage cap). Seeded synthetic instance generator. Ridge-regression shortfall forecast from weather/hour/weekday (Open-Meteo if reachable, otherwise a scenario file), labelled classical and clearly marked when the demand series is synthetic. Audit mode scores any schedule against quota, protected, max-consecutive and daily-hour rules and lists violations.
